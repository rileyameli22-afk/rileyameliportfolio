Wild Reach Creator ProgramGetting outdoor creators onto the industry's biggest trade show floor, and in front of the right brands

Outdoor Retailer wanted creators on the show floor. I built the program that got them there. 447 creators applied and 109 got in. I ran the roster across four tiers and matched each creator with 7 to 10 exhibiting brands based on fit, location, and audience. Adding a local tier for Minneapolis creators ended up being one of our best calls of the week.

The Weekly Trail MixA weekly insights newsletter for every Popfly brand customer

I write and send our weekly customer newsletter in our CEO's voice. Each edition takes one trend or opportunity in creator marketing and turns it into something a brand can act on that week. It ends with the Flyby Challenge, a small prompt to try something new on the platform.

  • Recent editions covered creator storefronts as an always-on retail model, Instagram Stories tracking, Summer of Adventure, Evergreens, and a swag bag opportunity for Outdoor Retailer brands.
  • Built a reusable HTML template and a voice guide, so every edition looks and sounds consistent.
